# Audio credits

Every recording here is in the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
taken from OpenGameArt.org. CC0 asks for no attribution; these are credited anyway. The files were cut, pitched and
re-encoded for the game with ffmpeg, as noted. The wind of a fall is not a recording: `src/audio/AudioEngine.ts`
makes it from filtered noise.

| File | Source | Author | What was done to it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ambient.mp3` | [Background space track](https://opengameart.org/content/background-space-track) (`MyVeryOwnDeadShip.ogg`) | yd | Seconds 8 to 222, with the last 6 crossfaded into the first 6 so it loops without a seam; loudness normalised to -23 LUFS. |
| `print.mp3` | [Electricity Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/electricity-sound-effects-0) (`continuousspark.wav`, `spark.wav`) | BMacZero | The continuous spark repeated for 0.85 s, then the single spark, with a short echo. |
| `reclaim.mp3` | as `print.mp3` | BMacZero | `print.mp3` reversed. |
| `creak1.mp3`, `creak3.mp3` | [27 Metal Audio Samples](https://opengameart.org/content/27-metal-audio-samples-sfx) (`metal_grit_02`, `metal_grit_01`) | Black Lodge Games, LLC | Slowed to about 40% speed (which drops the pitch), echo, low-pass at 3.2 kHz. |

| `impact1.mp3` | [100 CC0 metal and wood SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx) (`metal_slam_01.ogg`) | rubberduck | Slowed to 62% speed, short echo. |

| `land.mp3` | [Metal Impact Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/metal-impact-sounds) (`thud3.wav`) | BMacZero | Slowed to 70% speed, low-pass at 1.8 kHz. |
